# Coval

> Coval is the deployment-readiness platform for voice and chat AI agents: simulate conversations before launch, monitor real production calls, and improve reliability with metrics and human review. Supports inbound/outbound voice, standard chat, chat WebSocket, chat A2A, voice-to-voice (OpenAI Realtime, Gemini Live), SMS, WebSocket, Pipecat, and LiveKit agents.
